FOOD RECOMMENDATION

Hanoi is a culinary treasure trove where tradition meets street-side charm. As Vietnam’s capital, it’s also considered the country’s food capital, offering a rich tapestry of flavors shaped by history, climate, and culture. From the iconic phở bò (beef noodle soup) to the smoky allure of bún chả (grilled pork with noodles), every dish tells a story. Locals eat when they’re hungry, not by the clock, which means you’ll find sizzling grills and steaming bowls from dawn till late night.
Street food reigns supreme here—served on tiny plastic stools, often in alleyways or bustling corners. Hanoi’s cuisine is known for its balance: fresh herbs, delicate spices, and contrasting textures
